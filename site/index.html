What is ROADLINE '95?

ROADLINE '95 is a circuit racing game in the spirit of the racers of the mid-1990s - the years when texture-mapped 3D worlds, SVGA graphics and tracker music came to home computers. Every pixel of the 3D world is a number from one 256-color palette. Textures are point-sampled and affine-mapped, so they "swim" a little on big surfaces close to the camera, just like they did back then. Light, fog and headlights go through a colormap - a lookup table from color to color - exactly like the software engines of the time.

Under the hood it is modern: pure JavaScript and WebGL2, no libraries, and not a single image or sound file. Every texture, car, track, sample and song is generated by code when the game starts. The whole game fits in one HTML file of about 530 KB.

Features NEW!

  • Five tracks with tunnels, bridges and big hills - four circuits and one point-to-point sprint.
  • Six fictional sports cars, each with its own engine, gearbox, drive and handling - from a rally hatchback to a V12 grand tourer.
  • Seven AI rivals with their own cars and personalities: they overtake, defend, use your slipstream and make mistakes.
  • Game modes: single race, 5-round championship (win it to unlock a secret car!), time trial against your own ghost, and 2 players on one screen.
  • Day, dusk and night with headlights, lit windows and street lamps. Rain with wipers and spray, and fog.
  • TV-style replays of the whole race with trackside cameras, zoom and a helicopter.
  • Sound: engines that rev with your car, Doppler effect, tunnel echo, and four original songs played by a built-in tracker - switch songs with the radio key.
  • Visual damage: dents, scratches and broken lights.
  • Detail options just like in 1995: 320×240 or 640×480, flat or textured, perspective correction, draw distance, scenery density.
  • Records for every track, saved in your browser.
